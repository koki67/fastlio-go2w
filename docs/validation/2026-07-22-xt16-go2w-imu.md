# XT16 + Go2W IMU offline validation — 2026-07-22

## Verdict

The offline pipeline is **runnable**, but the current trajectory does **not**
meet the quality acceptance criteria. The adapter and runner processed the
original long3 bag to completion, isolated exactly the seven known timing-defect
frames, and generated the complete artifact set. However, the long3 trajectory
diverged after about 939 seconds and exceeded the translation-jump threshold
2,556 times. This result must not be treated as usable localization.

Corrected-driver recordings, absolute accuracy, Jetson execution, live-sensor
operation, and physical-robot operation remain **UNVERIFIED**.
